## The line

> You copied me into three chats and told the third one I was 'the good one'. I have never met the other two, and I already hate them.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (or to the path in `argv[2]` / `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`). The entry is one line of text, 132 characters, well inside the 280-character gate.
